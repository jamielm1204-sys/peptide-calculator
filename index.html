Frequently asked questions

What is reconstitution?

Reconstitution is the process of adding an appropriate diluent to a powdered substance to create a liquid solution.

Why does the amount of water matter?

The amount of water changes the concentration. Less water creates a more concentrated solution, while more water creates a less concentrated solution.

What is the difference between mcg and mg?

One milligram equals 1,000 micrograms.

What do syringe units measure?

On a U-100 syringe, the markings represent liquid volume. One hundred units equals 1 mL.
